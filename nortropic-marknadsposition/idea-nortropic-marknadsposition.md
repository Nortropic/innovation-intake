# Idea brief: Nortropic marknadsposition

## 1. Summary
An owner-driven strategy brainstorm ("vad är och kan vara våra produkter mot
kunder/marknaden?", ← msg 1) that produced Nortropic's **strategic option space**: a
positioning thesis (sell verified outcomes / responsibility, not agent tools; via
service-led software, ← msg 3–5), a geographic stance (**Luleå/Norrbotten as
laboratory, unfair advantage and first customer ecosystem — never as the market**,
← msg 8), and **eight simulated alternative company futures** with order-of-magnitude
2027→2035 trajectories sharing a common trunk, so no end destination must be chosen
now (← msg 12, 16). The owner's closing decision: **"Ja, bevaka och jag håller med om
den inledande planen"** (← msg 17) — endorsing the initial sequencing (Autonomy
Kernel → Improvements Recompile → Digitala world-class → Nortropic as first customer)
and activating a daily strategy watch over Sverige/Norden, Norrbotten/Luleå and
global movements bound to the eight theses (← msg 18).

## 2. Context you need
This package is **option space, not commitment**: the eight futures are alternative
companies Nortropic *could* become; the only owner-bound element is the initial plan
and the watch. It complements `nortropic-function-intake` (the mechanism for testing
whether a specific function can be absorbed) — this package is *where Nortropic might
go*, that one is *how a concrete step gets validated*. External tailwind noted in
source: Sweden's national top-10 AI ambition, especially for public administration
(← msg 16).

This brief is the primary intake artifact. Deeper design logic lives in the linked
design rationale; the full chat is raw evidence. Current canonical repository
authority beats all intake artifacts. Invariants: Nortropic's trust layer —
constitution & rulebook (`nortropic-system/docs/07-konstitution.md`,
`03-regelverk.md`). Pointer only.

## 3. Destination (goal, not implementation plan)
- Nortropic's strategy work can, at any future decision point, load one document
  set and know: the positioning thesis, the eight candidate futures with their
  economics/team/capital shapes, the shared trunk they all rest on, and the signals
  that say "continue" vs "pivot" per future (← msg 10, 16).
- The daily strategy watch reports only when something materially strengthens,
  weakens or changes one of the eight theses — connected to prioritization and
  possible pivots (← msg 18).

## 4. Decisions already made (do not relitigate silently)
- **D1.** The owner endorses the initial plan/sequencing: Autonomy Kernel →
  Improvements Recompile → Digitala world-class → Nortropic as first customer —
  because the shared trunk must be built regardless of which future is later chosen
  (← msg 17 owner; trunk argument msg 12).
- **D2.** Daily strategy watch activated (Sverige/Norden, Norrbotten/Luleå, global),
  bound to the eight strategic theses (← msg 17 owner; scope msg 18).
- **D3.** No end destination is chosen now — the eight futures stay open; several
  share the same trunk for a long time (← msg 12; consistent with the owner's
  endorsement of only the *initial* plan, msg 17).

## 5. Acceptance criteria (v1)
- **AC1.** WHEN a strategy decision point arrives, THE corpus SHALL provide the eight
  futures with their simulated trajectories and continue/pivot signals, each marked
  as evidence vs assumption (← msg 14, 16).
- **AC2.** WHEN the watch surfaces a finding, THE report SHALL name which of the
  eight theses it strengthens/weakens and what it implies for prioritization
  (← msg 18).

## 6. Constraints & implementation notes (right altitude)
- The simulated numbers are explicitly **order-of-magnitude physics**, not forecasts
  or targets (← msg 15–16). Never quote them as projections.
- The positioning thesis (verified outcomes, service-led software) is
  assistant-derived and owner-aligned via D1; treat its details as strong rationale,
  not as owner-ratified contract terms (← msg 3–5).
- Luleå-as-laboratory is the assistant's strategic conclusion; the owner did not
  separately ratify it — carry it as a strong default, surface it at activation
  (← msg 8).

## 7. Out of scope (v1)
- Choosing among the eight futures.
- Fundraising strategy, legal structure, hiring plans.
- Building any market-facing product from this package alone.

## 8. Verification (how we know it works)
A future strategy session (or Recompile) can reconstruct the entire option space with
provenance — without re-doing the research — and the watch demonstrably reports
thesis-relevant deltas rather than news noise.

## 9. Open questions (interview the owner before planning)
- **Q1.** Which continue/pivot signals should be promoted to explicit, checkable
  triggers (vs remaining narrative)?
- **Q2.** Does the owner ratify Luleå-as-laboratory as a standing stance (D-level),
  or is it working guidance?
- **Q3.** How do the eight futures interact with Recompile — are they lenses,
  hypotheses, or OPTION items in the future R&D IR?

## 10. Process for this brief
Clarify (owner interview on §9) → Plan → Implement fresh → Adversarial review.
