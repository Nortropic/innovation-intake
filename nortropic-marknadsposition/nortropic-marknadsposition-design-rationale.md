# Design rationale: Nortropic marknadsposition

Companion to `idea-nortropic-marknadsposition.md`. Derived independently from the
transcript `nortropic-marknadsposition-full-chat.md` (CONV-030 r1, 18 messages).

## 1. Core thesis
Nortropic should be defined by **what customers can hold it responsible for**, not by
its technology: sell verified outcomes through service-led software, keep the end
state open as eight alternative futures on one shared trunk, and use Luleå/Norrbotten
as laboratory and unfair advantage while addressing a much larger market from day one
(← msg 3–5, 8, 12).

## 2. Problem / current state / intended outcome
The owner asked the definitional question directly: what are/can be our products,
what are we, what can we become (← msg 1). Current state: deep internal architecture,
thin market definition. Intended outcome: a preserved, provenance-carrying strategic
option space plus one owner-endorsed initial path (← msg 12, 16–18).

## 3. Reasoning chain
1. Anthropic-startup-guide similarity prompts the market question (← msg 1).
2. Thesis forms: verified outcomes > agent tools; human judgment + hard gates as part
   of the product's trust story (← msg 3).
3. Commercial path: service-led software — managed services first, productize
   recurring capabilities, each stage funds and proves the next (← msg 4–5).
4. Geography pass (Sverige → Norrbotten → Luleå): competence scarcity + industrial
   transformation + public-sector AI ambition make the region a laboratory and
   credibility engine, not a market cap (← msg 6–8, 11).
5. Scenario deep-dive turns product ideas into **alternative company futures** with
   distinct capital/team/risk shapes; several share one trunk, so choosing now is
   unnecessary and premature (← msg 9–12).
6. Simulation makes the futures concrete as order-of-magnitude physics
   (30-Mkr service company vs 500-Mkr vertical operations vs platform scale)
   (← msg 13–16).
7. Owner endorses the initial plan and activates the watch (← msg 17–18).

## 4. Design decisions and why
- **Initial path endorsed (owner)** — the trunk (Kernel → Recompile → Digitala
  world-class → Nortropic as first customer) is required by every future, so building
  it defers no strategic option (← msg 12, 17).
- **Watch bound to theses (owner)** — a daily strategy watch that only reports
  thesis-relevant deltas keeps strategy current without owner attention waste
  (← msg 17–18).
- **Futures kept open** — the futures differ in capital, team, customer and founder
  life; evidence to choose does not exist yet (← msg 10, 12).

## 5. Explicit rejections / anti-requirements
- **Nortropic as a web agency / a bundle of AI agents / an internal automation
  system** — all three understate the company; rejected as self-definitions
  (← msg 5).
- **Luleå as the market** — rejected; it is the laboratory and first ecosystem, the
  market is global from the start (← msg 8).
- **Treating simulated numbers as forecasts** — explicitly disclaimed; they are
  order-of-magnitude physics for feeling the differences between futures
  (← msg 14–15).
- **Early generic horizontal "Organization OS" positioning** — the radar context that
  opened the adjacent thread (CONV-029 msg 1) carries the same skepticism; here the
  positioning runs through outcomes-first (← msg 3–5).

## 6. Explored but unresolved
- Which of the eight futures to pursue when the trunk is done (deliberately open,
  ← msg 12).
- Concrete continue/pivot signal definitions per future (narrative only, ← msg 10, 16).
- Capital strategy per future (sketched inside simulations, no owner position).

## 7. Important trade-offs / tensions
- **Keeping options open vs focus**: resolved for now by the shared trunk — the
  initial path builds what every future needs (← msg 12, 17).
- **Regional identity vs global ambition**: Luleå gives credibility, cases and talent
  scarcity leverage, but anchoring the brand regionally would cap the market — hence
  laboratory-not-market (← msg 8).

## 8. Metaphor / concept → technical principle
- "Nortropic mot Silicon Valley" (← msg 1) → ambition calibration: compare against
  frontier startup trajectories, not local agencies.
- "Alternativa företagsframtider" → strategy as simulated option portfolio with
  explicit evidence/assumption marking (← msg 10, 14).
